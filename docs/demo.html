Maintenance Exception Monitor

Property teams manage long queues of open maintenance requests. The expensive failures hide inside them: an emergency nobody dispatched, a vendor who never arrived, a resident left without updates, a repair nobody approved, or a gas smell logged as Low priority. This prototype finds those exceptions and hands a ranked list to a person to act on.

Synthetic data. All 25 requests are fictional. Prototype assumptions. Thresholds, categories and safety procedures are illustrative, not Lodg policies.

Human in the loop

Guardrails

The monitor surfaces and ranks exceptions. People make every operational decision.

  • Read-onlyChanges no data and makes no calls to vendors, residents or emergency services. A test enforces no network access.
  • Priorities are never changedMismatches go to a supervisor. An assigned Emergency is always handled as an Emergency.
  • Predefined safety proceduresGas, fire, flooding, electrical, elevator and structural issues show fixed steps written in advance, never generated.
  • Hazard words beat routine matchesIf a request mentions smell, smoke, sparking or pouring water without a confirmed safety category, a person reviews it.
  • No guessingUnfamiliar, ambiguous, mold and pest issues are sent for review instead of being assigned a category.
  • Tested against failureEight adversarial cases, one of which caught a probable gas leak misfiled as plumbing. Fixed, and now a regression test.
Roadmap

What I'd build next

  1. Validate with the operations team. Real thresholds, safety procedures reviewed by safety and legal, and a trial on a week of anonymized tickets.
  2. Read-only integration. Pull from the work-order system and send a daily digest to the ops channel. People still take every action.
  3. Alert state. Acknowledge and snooze flags, suppress repeats, and measure time-to-resolution for flagged items.
  4. Duplicate and repeat-problem detection. Spot the same unit and issue reported again.
  5. AI-assisted categorization. Suggestions a person confirms, for wording the keywords miss, measured against the challenge suite. Safety procedures stay predefined.
